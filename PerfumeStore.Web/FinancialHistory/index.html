About older records and corrections

Older records are reconstructed from their currently saved values. Their exact same-day order and earlier edits are unknown. The opening cash figure reconciles those records to the saved account balance and can include untracked earlier adjustments. It is not independently verified.

New cash entries show the day the balance changed (Jerusalem time). Profit entries stay in the sale or expense month; a later correction shows when it was recorded. Future edits add history entries rather than replacing earlier entries.
